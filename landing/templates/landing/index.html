What is the refund policy?

You have a 7-day refund window if the software does not work on your Windows 10/11 system. Contact us with your purchase email and a description of the issue.

Is MonsterCreative a desktop app?

Yes. MonsterCreative is a Windows desktop app built for Windows 10/11 64-bit systems.

Do I need a fal.ai account?

Yes. The app uses your own fal.ai API key for generation. That keeps usage billing transparent and avoids platform markup.

Are AI generation costs included?

No. The license covers MonsterCreative. AI usage is billed separately by fal.ai based on what you generate.

What happens after purchase?

You complete checkout securely, receive your license details by email, download the app, activate it, and connect your API key.

Can I use it for Arabic content?

Yes. MonsterCreative includes Arabic voice options for Gulf, Saudi, Egyptian, Maghrebi, and Standard Arabic use cases.

Does it replace an agency?

It replaces a lot of repetitive production work. Strategy, offer testing, media buying, and creative judgment still matter.

Can I transfer my license to a new device?

The license includes 1 device activation, but your license key is fully transferable if you change computers.
